// THE NARRATIVE

Engineering with Hands-on Grit

I am an Electronics & Communication Engineering undergraduate at Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College (GNDEC), Bidar, currently in my 5th semester maintaining a CGPA of 8.2 / 10.

My passion is centered around embedded systems, firmware development, and IoT architectures. I believe that true innovation occurs at the boundary where physical sensor measurements meet intelligent, memory-efficient firmware logic. Whether writing bare-metal C++ for an ESP32 or managing multi-sensor interrupt routines on an Arduino, I focus on hardware reliability, deterministic latency, and low power consumption.

Alongside hardware, I build responsive, production web portals — including official web platforms for institutions like Ace Techno Academy (acetechnoacademy.in) and departmental platforms for ECE and ASH at GNDEC Bidar.

In competitive technical research, I have won over ₹5,00,000 in research grants under the prestigious VTU VRIF SamShoDhana Scheme (2026) and Karnataka's NAIN 2.0 (Batch 1 & Batch 2). I also achieved a Top 5 placement in the Hardware Track at the KLE Belagavi Hackathon 2026, and earned dual institutional shortlists in the Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2025, 2026).
