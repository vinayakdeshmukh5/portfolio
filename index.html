// ABOUT & PHILOSOPHY

Engineering with Deterministic Precision

From low-level microcontrollers and register configurations to high-throughput cloud dashboards and user interfaces.

I am an Electronics & Communication Engineering undergraduate at GNDEC Bidar, driven by the challenge of connecting the physical real world with digital intelligence. My core focus lies in hands-on firmware development (C/C++, Arduino), microcontroller interfacing (ESP32, ESP8266, Uno), and real-world embedded prototyping.

Rather than treating software and hardware as isolated silos, I design complete feedback systems: from circuit schematics, sensor signal conditioning, and interrupt-driven firmware routines to responsive, accessible web portals that visualize real-time operational telemetry.

Beyond technical lab benches, I am deeply committed to engineering culture. I serve as the Student Coordinator for the ECE Department Newsletter, curating technical publications and student innovations, and hold active student memberships in IEEE and ISTE.
